Can you sound your horn to say hello?

You may give an audible warning only to avert imminent danger. You must not sound your horn to greet someone when there is no danger.

Can you hold your phone for navigation while driving?

You must not hold a mobile electronic device while driving, even to check directions. The rule applies whether you are making a call, reading a message or checking a map.
