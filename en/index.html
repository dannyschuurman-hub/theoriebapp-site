Legislation

Does waiting in your car count as parking?

Waiting for someone who is still indoors and is not getting in immediately counts as parking. Staying in the car does not change that.

Responsible driving

Can you hold your phone for navigation while driving?

You must not hold a mobile electronic device while driving, even to check directions. The rule applies whether you are making a call, reading a message or checking a map.

16 November 20262 min read

Priority and giving way

Turning right: does the cyclist beside you go first?

If you are travelling on the same road, let the cyclist go first. You are turning while the cyclist continues straight ahead. Using your indicator does not change the order.

16 November 20262 min read

Using the road

Can you pass on the right in queuing traffic?

In queuing traffic, you may pass a queue on the right. You do not have to stay behind the car to your left when your lane is moving faster.
