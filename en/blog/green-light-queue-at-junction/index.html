Green light and a queue: can you enter?

Theorie B editorial team · 28 November 2026 · 2 min read

In this article

Priority and giving way ↗Test yourself ↓

Short answer

You must not block a junction. If a queue would leave you stopped in the middle of it, wait before the junction, even when the light is green.

The light turns green. A line of cars stretches across to the far side of the junction. The last car in the queue is occupying the space you would need to pull into after crossing. You have room to move forward a little, but that would leave you stopped in the middle of the junction.

What the two articles say

A green light means proceed under RVV 1990, art. 68. Under RVV 1990, art. 14, drivers must not block a junction. Article 14 makes no exception for a green light.

Look for space beyond the junction

Follow the queue with your eyes to the far side. Is there enough room there for your whole car? If the only space you can see is before or on the junction, you have nowhere to join the queue without blocking the road that crosses yours. Wait before the junction until the queue on the far side has moved far enough for you to clear it completely.

What do hazard lights change?

Your car takes up the same amount of space with its hazard lights on. If you stop on the junction, you are still occupying the road that crossing traffic needs. Article 14 makes no exception for hazard lights either.

The ruleRVV 1990, art. 14
