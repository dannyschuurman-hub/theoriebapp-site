The Dutch car theory exam in English

If you are learning to drive in the Netherlands and Dutch is not your first language, you can prepare in English. This page explains what to expect and how to practise. Always check the current rules with the CBR; Theorie B is independent of the CBR.

Can I take the exam in English?

Yes. The CBR offers the car (category B) theory exam in English as well as in Dutch. You choose the language when you book. You book the exam yourself on the CBR website with DigiD.

The CBR changes the exam from time to time, so check the current exam page before you book.

What the exam looks like

The car theory exam is a computer exam about traffic rules and traffic situations. It has 50 questions that count and takes about 30 minutes. You pass with 44 or more correct answers.

What the exam covers

Traffic rules and situations by topic. Theorie B covers nine topics with 225 original practice questions: using the road, priority and giving way, special roads and manoeuvres, safe driving and emergencies, traffic signs, responsible driving, legislation, vehicle knowledge and driver assistance systems.

How to practise in English

Switch the whole app to English, or turn only the question into English when you want to compare it with Dutch. Every answer has an explanation and a source, so you learn the rule and not only the answer.

A mock exam in the app has 50 questions in 30 minutes, and you need 44 correct. Read the rules you keep missing in the magazine. A few examples are at the bottom of this page.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Dutch car theory exam available in English?

Yes. The CBR offers the car theory exam in English. You choose the language when you book.

Do I need to know Dutch road signs?

Yes, the signs are the Dutch ones. In the app, the explanations are in English.

How many questions do I need to get right?

You pass with at least 44 correct answers out of 50.

Is Theorie B affiliated with the CBR?

No. Theorie B is independent and has its own practice questions. Your score in the app says nothing about your CBR result.
