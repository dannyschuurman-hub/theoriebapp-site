Can you stop just after a zebra crossing?

You must not stop on a zebra crossing or within 5 metres of it. This applies on both sides, so also after the crossing.

Can you pass on the right in queuing traffic?

In queuing traffic, you may pass a queue on the right. You do not have to stay behind the car to your left when your lane is moving faster.

How to round up the time on a blue parking disc

You may round your arrival time up to the next hour or half-hour. If you arrive at 10:10, that means 10:30. You cannot choose any later time you like.
