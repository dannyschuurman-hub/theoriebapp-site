Minimum car tyre tread depth: how many millimetres?

Theorie B editorial team · 18 November 2026 · 3 min read

In this article

Vehicle knowledge ↗Test yourself ↓

Short answer

A car tyre must have at least 1.6 mm of tread in its main grooves around the entire circumference of the tread. Wear indicators are excluded from the measurement.

You look at a car tyre and see a clear groove. There seems to be plenty of tread left. Further round the tyre, you spot a shallower section. Which part counts? To check the minimum depth, examine the main grooves around the entire circumference of the tread.

Where does the 1.6 millimetre minimum apply?

A car tyre's main grooves must be at least 1.6 millimetres deep. Measure away from the wear indicators. The indicators themselves are excluded when determining the tread depth.

Check beyond the first section of tread you can see. A tyre goes all the way round. A groove that is deep enough in one place may be shallower elsewhere. One measurement does not tell you about that other section.

Pay attention to main grooves too. The rule specifies where the depth matters. Finding a deep groove somewhere on the tyre is not enough to complete the check.

An example on a tyre

Suppose a main groove is 2.5 millimetres deep at one point. You follow that groove further round the tyre. At another point, you measure just 1.0 millimetre.

That shallow section does not meet the minimum depth. The higher reading you took earlier does not change this. Both measurements come from the same main groove. You cannot skip the second point because the first looked good.

You do not calculate an average either. The deeper section cannot make up for the shallower one. Compare each measurement with the minimum and keep the whole circumference in mind.

The misconception: one good measurement is enough

A higher reading can be reassuring. At 2.5 millimetres, you may assume there is plenty of tread left. But that reading only describes the spot where you measured it. It tells you nothing about the rest of the tyre.

Keep the minimum depth separate from an individual reading. The minimum is the threshold you check against. A reading tells you the condition of the groove at one particular point.

Work through the check carefully: the main grooves, the depth away from the wear indicators, and the rest of the circumference. That way, the best-looking part of the tyre will not give you false reassurance.

The ruleRegeling voertuigen, art. 5.2.27 lid 4

Try it / Practice question

For a car tyre, what is the minimum tread depth in the main grooves, excluding the wear indicators?

The main tread grooves must be at least 1.6 mm deep around the full circumference. Wear indicators are excluded from this measurement.
